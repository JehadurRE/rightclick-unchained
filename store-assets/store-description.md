Built from the ground up for modern Google Chrome with 100% **Manifest V3 compliance**, zero external trackers, and zero telemetry.

---

### 🔥 Why RightClick Unchained?
With Google Chrome retiring legacy Manifest V2 extensions, beloved tools like *Absolute Enable Right Click & Copy* and *Allow Copy* stopped working or were disabled. Worse, many store alternatives are bloated with adware, tracking scripts, and dangerous data permissions.

**RightClick Unchained** was created to be the definitive, ultra-fast, open-source replacement. It runs entirely on your device, does not read your passwords, sends zero data to remote servers, and requires minimal permissions.

---

### ✨ Key Features

1. 🔓 **Universal Right-Click Enabler**
   Bypasses JavaScript `contextmenu` blockers. Restore your browser’s native right-click menu to inspect elements, save images, search Google, or use browser tools.

2. ✍️ **Universal Text Selection & Drag**
   Neutralizes `user-select: none !important`, `-webkit-user-select: none`, and hostile `selectstart` event preventions on paywalled blogs, documentation sites, university portals, and news archives.

3. 📋 **Copy & Cut Unblocker**
   Defeats hostile `copy` and `cut` listeners that intercept `Ctrl+C` or append unwanted promotional junk/tracking attributions to your clipboard.

4. 🔑 **Form & Password Paste Unblocker**
   Tired of banking and signup forms that block `Ctrl+V` or pasting complex 32-character passwords from your password manager? RightClick Unchained frees paste functionality across all inputs and forms.

5. 🎯 **Smart Text Lasso Grabber**
   Dealing with stubborn canvas text, locked tables, or SVG elements? Hit **Alt+Shift+X** to activate the Smart Lasso. Click any block or drag a selection box to instantly extract clean, unformatted plaintext straight to your clipboard!

6. ⚡ **Dual Engine Modes**
   - **Smart Mode (Recommended)**: Fixes 98% of sites without breaking web apps, custom video players, or interactive navigation menus.
   - **Absolute Mode**: Aggressively strips hostile overlay elements and drops all blocking listeners on extreme anti-copy sites.

7. 🌐 **Per-Domain Memory**
   Easily customize settings for individual websites or let it run globally. Your preferences are saved locally in your browser.

### ⌨️ Default Keyboard Shortcuts
- `Alt + Shift + U` — Open RightClick Unchained control panel
- `Alt + Shift + R` — Quick toggle unblock on current site
- `Alt + Shift + C` — Force copy active selection to clipboard

*(All shortcuts are fully customizable via chrome://extensions/shortcuts)*

### 🚀 How to Use
1. Install RightClick Unchained from the Chrome Web Store.
2. Pin the icon to your toolbar.
3. Visit any website that blocks right-click or text selection.
4. Enjoy unrestricted browsing! If a site uses extreme blockers, click the extension icon and switch to **Absolute Mode**.
